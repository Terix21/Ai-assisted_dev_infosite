# AI-Assisted Development Workshop Wiki

This page is automatically published to the repository’s GitHub Wiki when files in `wiki/` are pushed to `main`.

## Purpose

The workshop’s central idea is simple: AI can propose code, but a human remains accountable for what ships. Quality and security are reasons to verify the work before it is merged.

## Recommended session flow

1. Open the HTML presentation in a desktop browser.
2. Keep the presenter console on the private display.
3. Select **Launch presentation view** and move that window to the shared display.
4. Use the 45-minute timer target as a guide; reserve time for discussion.
5. End on **Discussion & Questions**, then share the QR-code materials slide.

## Facilitation notes

- Keep examples focused on small, reviewable changes.
- Treat AI output as a proposal that needs evidence, not as authority.
- Ask participants what they would need to know before approving a change.
- When policy questions arise, direct participants to their organization’s approved tools and processes.
- Use the repository checklist as a take-home reference rather than a presentation activity.

## Working agreement

Use this review sequence for AI-assisted changes:

**Define & Constrain → Generate → Test & Verify → Review & Commit**

The decision to commit or merge remains a human decision.
