Open this HTML directly in a modern browser. No installation, server or internet connection is needed. External resource links need internet only when opened.

Suggested pacing: about 45 minutes of teaching and 10 minutes of Q&A. Adjust the timer for your session. Go Deeper is optional.

The file opens in the presenter panel. Choose Launch presentation view to open the synchronized audience window, then move that window to the projected display. Keep this panel on your private display. Allow pop-ups for this file if your browser blocks the launch. Notes shown with N are visible in the audience window.

No analytics, cookies, remote fonts or external runtime libraries. Timers reset when the presenter panel reloads. Refreshing the presenter also reloads its connected presentation window. Reading view exposes all content for zoom, scrolling and assistive technology.
